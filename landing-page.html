What you gain from the B.Sc. in ICT

Six reasons students choose this programme, each backed by an official source.

Study every course in English

The programme is delivered entirely in English, so you practise listening, speaking, reading and writing in English throughout your degree.

Source: Studyportals programme page

Decide your specialty after a year of exploring

Build core ICT knowledge first. Each year the faculty holds track-selection sessions where instructors and senior students explain the eight tracks.

Source: ICT Mahidol news, 2024

Learn by building projects

A graduate describes the learning system as project-based, with internships, competitions and a working space provided by the faculty.

Source: ICT Mahidol alumni testimonial

Intern abroad and meet employers

The faculty offers internships abroad in countries such as Japan, Taiwan and Germany, plus IT competitions and an annual Job and Education Fair.

Source: ICT Mahidol admissions page

Start with classmates from China

The 2023–24 Mahidol listing shows Chinese students enrolled full time in the programme, so you join a group that already shares your language and background.

Source: Mahidol International Programs listing

Study at a ranked Thai university

ShanghaiRanking's ARWU 2026 places Mahidol first among Thai universities, in the 501–600 band. QS World University Rankings 2027 lists it at =345.

Sources: ShanghaiRanking, QS TopUniversities

Eight tracks in Year 2, and the careers they lead to

The B.Sc. in ICT leans towards software and also teaches business and management.

Tracks you can choose in Year 2

One degree, eight ways to specialise.

  • Computer Science
  • Database and Intelligent Systems
  • Computer Networks
  • Software Engineering
  • Electronic Business Systems
  • Multimedia Systems
  • Management Information Systems
  • Health Information Technology

Track names follow the faculty's 2022 to 2024 sessions. Later handbooks also mention Artificial Intelligence, so confirm the current list with the faculty.

Jobs the faculty lists for graduates

Roles named in the faculty's own admissions FAQ.

  • Programmer or software developer
  • IT specialist
  • Systems analyst or designer
  • Web designer or developer
  • Database administrator
  • Network or system administrator
  • IT security analyst
  • IT or e-business manager
  • Quality assurance analyst

The faculty explains that the B.Sc. in ICT focuses more on software, while Computer Engineering focuses more on hardware. If you want a software career alongside business and management knowledge, ICT is the programme built for it.

Source: ICT Mahidol admissions FAQ

How to apply for the August 2027 intake

Applications run in four rounds. Check the schedule, follow three steps, then wait for your interview.

Source: ICT International Direct Admissions 2027. Dates can change, so check the page before you apply.

  1. Read the requirements and prepare your documents

    • High school diploma or equivalent, certified by the Ministry of Education (.pdf)
    • Transcript or other proof of academic performance (.pdf)
    • First page of a valid passport, for international students (.pdf)
    • One 1-inch photo taken within the last 6 months (.jpg or .jpeg, up to 150 KB)
    • Resume, if you have one (.pdf)
    • Statement of purpose (essay), up to 2 A4 pages (.pdf)
    • Math test score that meets the Faculty’s criteria (.pdf)
    • English test score that meets the Faculty’s criteria (.pdf)
    • Achievements and certificates, if you have any (.pdf)

    Accepted scores include Gaokao Math 70 or higher and Gaokao English 100 or higher. IELTS 5.0 and TOEFL iBT 60 also qualify. Test scores must be from the last two years, and the minimum high school GPA is 2.50 or equivalent. See the full list of accepted tests.

  2. Learn how to apply

    The Faculty’s step-by-step guide shows how to register, fill in the online form, upload your documents and pay the 600 THB application fee.

  3. Wait for the interview announcement

    The Faculty announces which applicants are eligible for the interview exam, and you receive an email. Then you take the online interview.
